Duration
A maximum of 20 working days are provided → This is not equivalent to a full month or 4 weeks. It may be less, and any unused days will be forfeited.



Recharge leave rewards loyalty and ensures proper rest. To maintain its purpose, RECHARGE must be taken in one continuous period, not in multiple phases.




 Eligibility
Earned after 5 years: After 5 years of continuous employment with the Company, starting from your main contract, you're eligible for Recharge leave.
Who qualifies?: All employees with a main contract, no matter the hours worked.
Part-time? No worries: Your entitlement stays the same, no matter if you're part-time.
Maternity/paternity leave: While the time spent on maternity/paternity leave doesn’t count towards your 5-year total, all the time before and after adds up!
When can’t you take it?: If you're in the process of leaving (except for transitioning to another entity), Recharge leave isn’t available.


 Utilization
Paid leave: You’ll receive your regular salary, and you're still eligible for your annual bonus.
Vacation: Taking this leave won’t affect your vacation entitlement.
Annual Bonus: Your annual bonus base stays the same during the leave.
Timing: You have 24 months to take the leave from when you're eligible.
No timing agreement? If you and your manager can’t agree on when to take it, a small payout might be possible (but it needs approval from the EIT CPO and CEO).
Process
Eligibility & Notifications 📅
Manager’s role: Managers know what their team needs and what is the workload.
Employee’s role: Check your entitlements on the HR EGJE Portal.





Planning the Long-Term Leave ✈️
Agree & Plan: The employee and manager must agree on when to take the leave within 12 months of the entitlement date. This is entered into the attendance system in EGJE.
If there’s no agreement: The issue is escalated to HR, who will check the reasons why the leave hasn’t been planned.
Final Decision: If needed, the head of HR can approve financial compensation as a last resort.


During the Long-Term Leave 🏖️
Salary: Employees continue to receive their basic salary, and they’re still eligible for their annual bonus.
Next Entitlement: After the leave, the EGJE Portal will update with the date for the next Recharge entitlement in 5 years.
